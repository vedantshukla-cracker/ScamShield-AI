    Knowledge Armor

    Top 4 Modern Scam Vectors

    Understand the core deceptive mechanisms cybercriminals deploy across channels.

    🚨

    Digital Arrest & Coercion

    Scammers pose as Customs, Police, or CBI officials over Skype/WhatsApp claiming parcels contain illicit goods, coercing victims into "clearance payments".

    Golden Rule: Real law enforcement NEVER conducts court hearings or arrests over video calls.
    🏦

    Bank KYC & Electricity Cut

    Urgent threats claiming electricity will be disconnected tonight or SIM/Pan card is blocked unless an APK app or verification link is processed.

    Golden Rule: Utility companies never shut off power via automated SMS with personal mobile contact numbers.
    📈

    Pig Butchering / High Returns

    Fraudsters build romantic or friendly rapport over weeks, then lure victims to fraudulent trading apps showing fake exponential crypto profits.

    Golden Rule: Guaranteed 20%+ weekly returns do not exist. Any app demanding "tax fees" to withdraw is a trap.
    📦

    Smishing & Parcel Redelivery

    Fake SMS claiming package delivery failed due to missing street number. Victims are asked to pay a nominal fee ($1 or ₹25), harvesting credit card credentials.

    Golden Rule: Track deliveries directly inside official postal/courier apps, never through SMS links.
    🚨 Immediate Crisis Response

    Already Clicked a Link or Sent Money?

    Act within the golden 2-hour window to minimize financial and identity exposure.

    01

    Freeze Accounts & Cards

    Open your bank app immediately and toggle temporary card freeze or call your bank's 24/7 emergency fraud helpline.

    02

    Reset Critical Passwords

    Change your email, banking, and social credentials from a separate, secure device. Terminate all active sessions.

    03

    Report to Cyber Police

    File a report with national cyber helplines immediately. Financial transactions can often be put on lien if caught quickly.
